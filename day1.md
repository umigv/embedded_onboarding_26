## PCB & Design

Best way to learn this is through practice. Through our previous members, they recommend taking EECS 300 or 215 as a base step. 
Next best thing is the direct Altium tutorial, which has a front to end design process done in a short amount of time to creating your first PCB. Follow this, and use the Altium Onboarding file as a reference. One completed PCB is all that is needed for this onboarding project. Show your work to any of the leads or Chihyun to show your completion.

## Lidar FPGA

EECS 270 is Highly Recommended, but most concepts can be learned from HDLBits. Go through the Lidar FPGA onboarding document and go around Dev and he will walk through the project & give a general overview of FPGAs.
